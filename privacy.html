Privacy policy

Receipts Uploader

Last updated 5 November 2026

Receipts Uploader is used only by its owner, to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

  • What it accesses: the YouTube Data API permission to upload videos (youtube.upload). It does not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, subscribers or analytics.
  • What it stores: the Google sign-in token for each of the owner's channels, kept only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server.
  • What it shares: nothing. No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Revoking access: remove "Receipts Uploader" at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time.

Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
